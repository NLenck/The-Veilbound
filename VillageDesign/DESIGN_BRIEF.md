# Veilbound ruined village concept v1

Preview created with the built-in image-generation tool on 28 October 2026. This is concept artwork, not a screenshot of an implemented map. Original user sketch and current game assets were left unchanged.

## Layout authority

The user's sketch defines the layout. Five houses, one church, one hall and one tavern occupy eight separate footprints. Two houses sit northwest; church and one house northeast; hall and tavern southwest; two houses southeast. A broad north-south road crosses a narrower east-west road. Keep the central junction clear. The outer circle is a playable boundary, not a constructed wall.

## Art direction

Matte low-poly dark fantasy: ash-grey broken masonry, weathered brown timber, grey cobbles, desaturated moss and earth, dead trees, dark barrels and angular crimson veil crystals. Interiors remain readable from the gameplay camera. Church: damaged bell-gable, pointed windows, altar and broken pews. Hall: broad civic ruin, dais, benches and faded banners. Tavern: bar, hearth, barrel storage and ruined tables. Houses: smaller domestic interiors and hearths.

## Implementation requirements

- Match the sketch's wall breaks with floor-level passable openings. Side rubble must not seal these gaps.
- Provide multiple routes into and through each building, with adequate width for large enemies.
- Validate actual collision, navigation and swept movement; the image cannot certify path clearance.
- Keep trees, barrels and crystals out of the principal road and opening clearances.
- Use camera-friendly ruined walls and cutaways; avoid roofs that conceal combat.
- Preserve the existing village until the new map is ready for review.
